How it works

Three steps. Zero effort.

Drop waste into the top. The sensor reads the material, the motor rotates the drum, and the hatch does the rest — all inside a single compact cylinder.

1

Drop it in

The top sensor instantly reads and identifies the material you're dropping — plastic, metal or paper.

2

AI sorts it

A silent motor and precision gear train rotate the drum to align the correct compartment with the top hatch.

3

It locks it in

The hatch opens at the right slot, the waste drops into its compartment, and a light + tone confirms the sort.
